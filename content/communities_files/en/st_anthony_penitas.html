St Anthony of Padua

Peñitas, Texas

St Anthony of Padua Catholic Church in Peñitas is a mission community of Our Lady Queen of Angels Parish in La Joya.

The church serves Catholic families living in Peñitas and nearby communities and gathers the faithful for Mass, prayer, and parish activities.

The mission is dedicated to St Anthony of Padua, a Franciscan priest known for his preaching and love for the poor.
