Our Lady Queen of Angels

La Joya, Texas

Our Lady Queen of Angels Catholic Church is the main parish church serving the Catholic community of La Joya, Texas, in the Diocese of Brownsville. The parish also serves several mission communities in the surrounding areas.

The parish celebrates Mass in English and Spanish and offers many ministries, faith formation programs, and pastoral services for families throughout the region.

Mission Communities

  • St Anthony – Peñitas
  • St Mary Magdalene – Abram
  • St Joseph – Havana
