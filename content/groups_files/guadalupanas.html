Las Guadalupanas

Las Guadalupanas are a group devoted to Our Lady of Guadalupe, the Patroness of the Americas. Their mission is to promote devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary and to serve the parish through prayer, faith formation, and charitable works.

The group plays an important role in organizing celebrations for the Feast of Our Lady of Guadalupe on December 12 and participates actively in parish liturgical and community events.

Activities

  • Rosary and Marian prayers
  • Organization of the Feast of Our Lady of Guadalupe
  • Support of parish celebrations
  • Charitable and community activities

Women of the parish who wish to deepen their devotion to Our Lady and serve the community are invited to join.
