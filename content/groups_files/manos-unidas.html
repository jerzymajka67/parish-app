Manos Unidas de Abram

Manos Unidas de Abram is a parish group dedicated to supporting the spiritual and material needs of the parish community. The name "United Hands" reflects their commitment to working together in faith and service.

Members collaborate in organizing parish events, supporting charitable initiatives, and assisting families in need within the community.

Activities

  • Community service projects
  • Support for parish events
  • Charitable initiatives for families in need
  • Promotion of unity and cooperation within the parish

Anyone interested in serving the community and working together in faith is welcome to participate.
