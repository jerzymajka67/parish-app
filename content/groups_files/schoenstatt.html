Schoenstatt Movement

The Schoenstatt Movement is a Catholic apostolic movement dedicated to deepening faith and strengthening the spiritual life of families and individuals through a special devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Members gather regularly for prayer, formation, and reflection on how to live the Gospel in daily life. Inspired by the spirituality of Schoenstatt, the group seeks to grow in trust in God, commitment to the Church, and service to others.

Activities

  • Monthly prayer meetings
  • Marian devotion and consecration
  • Faith formation and spiritual reflection
  • Support for families and parish life

New members are always welcome. If you are interested in learning more about the Schoenstatt spirituality, please contact the parish office.
