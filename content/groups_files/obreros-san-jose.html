Obreros de San José

The Obreros de San José (Workers of St. Joseph) is a group inspired by the example of Saint Joseph, the patron of workers and families. The group promotes hard work, responsibility, and faithful service to God and the Church.

Members support parish activities and help with practical projects that contribute to the life and maintenance of the parish community.

Activities

  • Support for parish maintenance projects
  • Community service and volunteer work
  • Prayer and devotion to Saint Joseph
  • Support for parish events

Men who wish to serve the parish community through work and faith are invited to participate.
