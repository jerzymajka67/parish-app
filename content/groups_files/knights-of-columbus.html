Knights of Columbus

The Knights of Columbus is a Catholic fraternal organization founded on the principles of charity, unity, fraternity, and patriotism. Members support the Church, the parish community, and families through service and charitable work.

The Knights are actively involved in parish life and frequently organize fundraisers, charitable initiatives, and volunteer projects that benefit the parish and the wider community.

Activities

  • Charitable fundraising events
  • Support for parish programs
  • Community service
  • Faith and family activities

Catholic men who wish to grow in their faith while serving the Church and the community are invited to join the Knights of Columbus.
