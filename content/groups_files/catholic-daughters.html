Catholic Daughters

The Catholic Daughters of the Americas is one of the largest organizations of Catholic women in the United States. The group is dedicated to strengthening faith, promoting charity, and supporting the mission of the Church.

Members work together to serve the parish and community through charitable projects, prayer, and social activities that foster friendship and faith.

Activities

  • Charitable projects
  • Support for parish programs
  • Faith formation and prayer
  • Community outreach

Women of the parish who wish to grow in faith and serve others are welcome to join the Catholic Daughters.
